Our Story

From Ukraine,
with love for Dundee

Sasha's Corner was opened by Sasha and her husband — a young Ukrainian couple who arrived in Dundee carrying their homeland's tradition of heartfelt hospitality: no guest ever leaves without something warm in hand and a smile on their face.

Step inside and you'll find a boutique hideaway dressed in twinkling lights, hanging greenery and little magical corners made for lingering — and for photographs. Every matcha is whisked to order, every bubble tea shaken with care.

  • Ceremonial-grade matcha whisked fresh for every cup
  • Pet-friendly — water bowls & treats always on the house
  • A little magic in every corner, made for slow afternoons
